FAQs and Contact

FAQs

Why is my payout delayed?
Most delays happen because a payout is still under verification, your bank details were updated recently, or the transfer is waiting on the bank's processing window.
How do I get a product listing approved faster?
Use a clear title, accurate price, real product images, and complete specifications. If a listing is still under review after 24 hours, raise a ticket with the listing ID.
What should I include in an urgent order issue?
Include the order ID, customer name, issue type, and any tracking or proof of shipment. That helps support escalate it to the right team faster.
