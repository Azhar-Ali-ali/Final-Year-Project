percent Commission Settings

Configure the marketplace commission percentage and save your changes for future orders.

Commission must be between 0 and 100.

info Commission Information
  • Commission is deducted only from the product price.
  • Shipping charges are paid by the customer.
  • Shipping charges are not deducted from the seller.
  • Changes apply only to future orders.
  • Existing orders keep the commission rate saved at the time the order was created.
  • Sellers cannot modify the commission.
  • Customers never see the commission.
help What is Marketplace Commission?

The marketplace commission is the percentage deducted from the product price before calculating the seller's earnings. It is configured by the Admin and is automatically applied to all future orders.
